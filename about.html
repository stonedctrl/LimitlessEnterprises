Who We Are:

Limitless Enterprises started as a for-profit marketing agency. Then we noticed something we couldn't ignore: the industry we worked in was full of gatekeepers. Talent was everywhere. Opportunity wasn't. In 2017, we restructured as a non-profit. Today, we're a team of 12 full-time creatives and over 30 volunteer industry professionals dedicated to one thing — making sure that where you grew up doesn't determine how far you go.

What We Do:

We work with young artists aged 16–25 from low-income communities across Johannesburg. We build their brands, develop their public image, connect them with industry contacts, and give them the kind of professional support that used to only be available to people who could afford it.
